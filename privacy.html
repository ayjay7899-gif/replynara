Privacy information

This page explains our current concept site's limited operation. It is not a final privacy notice for a live service. The operating person's legal identity and required contact details must be confirmed before public commercial launch.

What the website does

The website is a static demonstration. It has no enquiry form, visitor account, payment checkout, customer database or first-party analytics. The fictional examples run locally in your browser.

When you email us

If you contact cleardesk.services.uk@gmail.com, your email address and message will be received in Gmail. We use the message to read and respond to your general enquiry, not to automatically enrol you in marketing. Do not send your customers’ personal data, payment details or sensitive information.

Legal basis and retention

We expect to rely on legitimate interests to respond to general business enquiries where appropriate, subject to completing the required assessment. A documented retention period and any additional service-specific legal basis must be finalised before launch. You can request access, correction, deletion where applicable, or object by contacting the address above.

Third-party services

Website hosting and the email provider may process necessary technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and messages. Their applicable terms and privacy information also apply.

Before operational launch

We must identify the responsible legal operator, publish full contact details, document data uses and retention, assess any ICO fee requirement, and put agreements and security controls in place for any actual customer information.
